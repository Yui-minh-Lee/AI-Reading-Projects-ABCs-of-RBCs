# Prompt: Clarify Confusing Section

Use medium reasoning.

Read `AGENTS.md` first.

I am confused about this section:

`[SECTION_OR_QUOTE]`

Please explain it like a teacher.

Context:

- Chapter note: `ChapterNotes/[CHAPTER_NOTE_FILE]`
- Source chapter file, if needed: `Sources/Chapters/[CHAPTER_FILE]`

Goals:

1. Explain the idea in clear Chinese.
2. Preserve important English terms.
3. Connect it to the chapter's main argument.
4. Give a concrete example if useful.
5. Explicitly explain the missing bridge: starting condition, mechanism, intermediate step, conclusion, and caveat when useful.
6. Do not over-expand into unrelated topics.
7. If a chart/table/formula is involved and PDF text may not be enough, say so clearly.

If I ask you to write the explanation back into the note, add a short clarification box near the relevant section:

> 💡 Clarification
> [explanation]

Rules:

- Do not rewrite the whole note unless explicitly asked.
- Do not create separate glossary, concept, formula, or txt extraction files.
- Do not modify source files.
- Do not screenshot or embed figures unless explicitly asked.
