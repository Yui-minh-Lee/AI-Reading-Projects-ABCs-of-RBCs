## Recommended first-pass route

Use the sequence:

1. Introduction, for orientation.
2. Chapter 1, for the Solow base and first log-linear approximation.
3. Chapter 3, for infinitely lived agents and representative-agent foundations.
4. Chapters 4-5, for recursive deterministic and stochastic dynamic programming.
5. Chapter 6, for Hansen's RBC model and log-linear solution methods.
6. Chapter 7, for linear quadratic dynamic programming.
7. Part Two selectively, depending on whether the target is monetary models, New Keynesian rigidity, policy rules, or open-economy models.
